# NEXUS Cozy Cyber-Cat + Starry Twilight Design System

## 1. Aesthetic Atmosphere
Inspired by cozy late-night lo-fi gamer setups and anime twilight skies:
- **Night Skies**: `#090714` (Deep void) -> `#1a103c` (Violet nebula) -> `#4c1d95` (Horizon twilight) -> `#f59e0b` (Warm city lamp glow).
- **Stars & Constellations**: HTML5 Canvas rendering sparkling 4-pointed stars `✦`, twinkling dots, a glowing crescent moon `🌙`, drifting translucent clouds, and shooting stars (meteors) whenever a task is completed.
- **Atmosphere Modes**:
  1. `🌌 Twilight Night` (Animated starry sky canvas with shooting stars)
  2. `💜 Cyber Cozy` (Deep purple neon cyberpunk glass)
  3. `🪟 Übersicht Transparent` (Pure transparent canvas for macOS Desktop Übersicht widget)

## 2. Signature Indicator Brackets
Cards have a 3.5px solid left bracket with 10px rounded corners:
- `critical / group`: `#ef4444` left border + dark burgundy glow (`rgba(127, 29, 29, 0.35)`)
- `home / active`: `#22c55e` vibrant neon emerald
- `finance / bill`: `#f59e0b` warm amber glow
- `cyber / study`: `#a855f7` neon purple with soft glow
- `wishlist / question`: `#64748b` slate steel bracket

## 3. Cyber Cat Mascot «Нексус» (0_o)
- Procedurally animated SVG cat with neon-violet cat-ear headphones and expressive glowing eyes (`0_o`, `^_~`, `-_-`).
- Reactive cursor tracking (eyes follow mouse coordinates).
- Purring interaction on click with floating pixel hearts (`💜`) and randomized words of encouragement for Egor and Anya.
- Couple XP bar (`LEVEL UP ↑`) with unlocked cosmetics (HSE/RUDN glasses, Leader crown, cyber-scarf).
